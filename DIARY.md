## 2026-08-11 — It's alive!

The first version of BellaChat is finished and on GitHub. It has accounts,
1-on-1 chats, group rooms with join codes, emojis, and pictures. Next step:
deploy it on Render and give my friends the link.

## 2026-08-10 — Why I started this

I wanted a way to chat with my friends without WeChat or WhatsApp, which are
usually banned by parents. So I'm building my own — simple, private, just for
us. If we make it ourselves, we understand it and it's ours.
